Conferences

Conference presentations, invited sessions, and scientific committees

Conferences · Invited Sessions · Scientific Committees

More than two decades of contributions to national and international conferences — from COMPSTAT 2002 in Berlin to ENBIS 2026 in Florence — on bibliometrics and science mapping, tree-based and explainable machine learning, text analysis, and health and tourism analytics.

Active participant in numerous national and international conferences — DSSR, SDS, ASA, COMPSTAT, IFCS, CLADAG, JADT, ECDA, ENBIS, and CMStatistics — where I have presented research contributions related to bibliometric analysis, classification methods, explainable machine learning, and methodological innovation in the social sciences. Invited seminars and doctoral lectures are listed on the Teaching page.
